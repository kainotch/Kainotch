Between 2025 and 2026, I found myself picking up and experimenting with random programming languages—often with no specific project requirement in mind.

Some people recommend sticking strictly to one language until you master it. While focus is important, jumping between different paradigms taught me how languages think:

1. **Kotlin / Java:** Strong typing, object orientation, and Android ecosystem patterns.
2. **TypeScript / JavaScript:** Event-driven architecture and asynchronous flows.
3. **Rust / Go:** Memory safety, concurrency, and minimalist syntax.

Don't be afraid to experiment just for the fun of learning!
